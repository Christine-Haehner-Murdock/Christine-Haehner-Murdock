# Christine Hähner-Murdock

**Independent AI-safety researcher · Lecturer**

One subject throughout: **people and what they do** — studied in social theory, taught in classrooms, painted on canvas, tested under a barbell, and researched in human–AI trust.

## Research

I work on longitudinal, evidence-based trust signals between humans and AI systems: what authenticated interaction history can and cannot establish, why intent cannot be read but history can be evidenced, and why false positives are a first-class safety cost — they teach evasion and destroy the very evidence better judgment would need.
